What the product does

Five modules that do first-line analyst work.

Module 1

Alert triage agent

Groups and de-duplicates alerts, adds threat intelligence and asset context, scores priority and says why.

Analysts can override any score. Every decision is logged.

Module 2

Investigation agent

Pulls related logs and events, builds an incident timeline, maps behaviour to MITRE ATT&CK techniques and drafts a plain-language finding.

Findings stay drafts until an analyst confirms them.

Module 3

Guided response

Suggests containment steps such as isolating a host, disabling an account or blocking an indicator, as playbooks.

Nothing runs without explicit approval and role-based permission.

Module 4

Compliance assistant

Collects evidence for annual risk assessments and drafts incident notifications aligned to Cyber Security Act timelines.

Drafts only. The customer files the notification.

Module 5

Analyst copilot

Lets staff ask questions about their alerts and incidents in English or Bahasa Malaysia.

Answers cite the events they come from.

Works with

Your existing tools

Connects through standard APIs and log formats to SIEM, endpoint detection, firewall, email security and cloud audit logs. It does not replace them.

Design principles

Built around Malaysian requirements.

  • Your data stays yours. Each customer is isolated. A Malaysian-region hosting option is planned for customers who require it. Customer data will not be used to train shared models.
  • Explainable. Every conclusion links back to the events behind it, which also helps audit.
  • Local compliance. Reporting templates for the Cyber Security Act 2024 and the Personal Data Protection Act 1998.
  • Local fit. Bilingual interface, local support hours and pricing sized for mid-sized organisations and regional managed security providers.
